## Design Specificity Verdict

Grounded and product-specific. Two-register ground derived from 18 party colours the app does
not own; transport bar height feeds the chamber's max-height; continuous-vs-discrete motion rule
enforced through one shared hook. Lapses in sections 05 and 06, which fall back on generic
chart-page vocabulary (native select + the app's only bordered buttons).

Deterministic scan: 1 CLI finding across the whole app (side-tab, verification.css:12). Browser
injection at 1440x900 and 380x780: 7 and 6 findings, console clean at both. First detector run
was silently degraded (missing HTML parser modules, regex fallback); re-run at full fidelity after
installing 4 packages into the skill directory.

False positives: gradient-text on body (misfires on rail.css mask-image). side-tab contested, not
clean - documented state marker, also carried by a shape glyph.

No user-visible overlay produced: live-server flow deliberately skipped, detect-url.mjs unusable
(requires puppeteer, absent).

## What's Working

1. The verification line distinguishes three states and refuses to claim success on placeholder
   data. The decision not to desaturate data on failure is documented in the component.
2. The metric strip's subgrid: five shared rows make scanning a straight horizontal read.
3. The legend's split at seats/no-seats states the threshold's effect rather than requiring the
   reader to reconstruct it; hatch is a pattern, so it is the accessibility answer too.
