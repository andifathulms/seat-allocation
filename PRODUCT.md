# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

A literate non-specialist who knows the 4% threshold exists and has never seen what it
does: a journalist, a student, a caleg's staffer, a civil servant reading the RUU Pemilu.

The app must be understandable with no political science background, and must not require
reading anything before touching a control. Someone should be able to arrive, move one
control, and learn something true.

## Product Purpose

Perform, in front of the reader, the two rules that turn Indonesian votes into DPR seats:
the 4% national threshold (UU 7/2017 Pasal 414) and Sainte-Laguë highest averages applied
across 84 dapil (Pasal 415). Reproduce the certified 2024 result exactly, then let anyone
change the rules and watch 580 seats recompose on the same fixed votes.

Success is a reader who can state what the threshold did in 2024 and what a different
figure would have done, and who can follow every number back to its KPU source.

The app serves two jobs at once, and neither may be sacrificed for the other:

1. A working civic instrument for the audience above, usable during the RUU Pemilu
   deliberation.
2. A demonstration piece of the author's engineering and design work.

Where the two pull apart, the civic instrument's correctness and restraint win, because a
tool that overstates itself fails as a portfolio piece too.

## Positioning

Mahkamah Konstitusi Putusan 116/PUU-XXI/2023 held the 4% threshold constitutional for the
2024 election only, and conditionally constitutional thereafter: the legislature must
reformulate it before the 2029 electoral stages begin, and the Court attached conditions,
among them that any new figure preserve the proportionality of a proportional system and
minimise votes that cannot be converted into seats.

The Court has, in effect, ordered a calculation. This app performs that calculation on
certified data, transparently, and lets anyone else perform it. The differentiator is not
the visualisation but the verifiability: a pure engine, a reproduction gate that blocks
deploy, and a trace object that the "show your work" view renders rather than recomputes,
so the display and the computation cannot drift apart.

## Operating Context

- Read on a laptop or a phone, often on the first visit, with no account and no onboarding.
- Argued about and linked: every configuration serialises to the query string, so a claim
  about a 2% threshold can be sent as a URL.
- Consulted alongside the source documents themselves. Citation popovers carry the pasal
  text and are dismissible and keyboard-reachable, because people will want to read them.
- No backend, no analytics, no runtime fetch to any origin but the app's own static JSON.
  Deployed as a static site to GitHub Pages.

## Capabilities and Constraints

**Confirmed capabilities.** Party-level allocation across all 84 dapil under four rule
knobs (threshold 0-10%, threshold scope, divisor method, geography) and five metrics.
Five instruments: chamber, archipelago, vote bar, divisor cascade, proportionality plot.
Every rule cites its pasal; every figure cites its KPU document.

**Out of scope, deliberately.** Candidate-level allocation (Indonesia's open list needs a
caleg dataset an order of magnitude larger and adds nothing to an argument about rules);
2019 and 2014 rule packs (deferred, but the data model must not make them hard to add);
malapportionment; DPD and DPRD; any forecast of a future election.

**Hard technical constraints.** The engine is pure and imports nothing. Full recomputation
under 16 ms so the threshold scrubber runs on every frame with no debounce and no worker.
Total bundle plus data under 1 MB. No backend.

**Terminology.** Dapil (electoral district), kursi (seat), ambang batas (threshold),
suara sah (valid votes), nomor urut (ballot number). Party names exactly as KPU writes
them. Indonesian number formatting throughout: `151.796.631`, `4,0%`.

**Data state, open.** `public/data/dapil-2024.json` is a placeholder generated by
`scripts/extract/build-dapil.ts`, not a certified recapitulation, and says so at its root.
Obtaining the certified dapil × party table **is an active goal**. Future work must keep
the provenance switch, the reproduction gate and the "not reproduced" header wired and
ready, and must treat the current state as temporary rather than designing around it as
permanent. Dropping the certified file in place with `"provenance": "certified"` turns the
gate green and switches the header with no code change.

**Locale, confirmed.** Indonesian only. The second English locale contemplated in
CLAUDE.md is not shipping; `strings.id.ts` is the whole copy surface.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: **Suara ke Kursi**. Not to be renamed to an evocative single word. English
  descriptor when one is needed: "How Indonesian votes become parliamentary seats."
- A brand mark exists: a hemicycle of seat dots on a chamber floor, with a teal cluster
  marking the seats a rule admits or discards. Teal specifically because no DPR party owns
  it, so the accent can never be misread as a party. The mark must never be tinted with a
  party colour, and its dot order must never imply a real standing.
- Voice: describe the arithmetic, never the outcome. No exclamation marks, no rhetorical
  questions, no second-person exhortation. "Sepuluh partai tidak memperoleh kursi" is
  correct; "sepuluh partai kehilangan haknya" is not. This is enforced in review and
  extends to comments, commit messages and variable names.
- The author credit in the footer is a personal signature and is kept visually separate
  from the legal and source attribution.

## Evidence on Hand

- `public/data/parties-2024.json` — certified national valid votes for all 18 parties,
  summing to 151.796.631 exactly.
- `public/data/official-2024.json` — the 580 seats of Keputusan KPU 1206/2024.
- `public/data/rules-2024.json` — pasal citations with source text and URLs for UU 7/2017,
  Putusan MK 116/PUU-XXI/2023, PKPU 6/2023 and Keputusan KPU 1206/2024.
- `public/data/dapil-2024.json` — **placeholder, not certified.** See above.
- `public/brand/` — favicon, app icons, maskable icon and share card.
- Measured, not claimed: 40 engine tests with hand-computed expected values; 0,23 ms
  median full recomputation; zero axe-core violations at three widths in both colour
  schemes.

**Must not be fabricated.** Per-dapil vote figures, any 2019 or 2014 result, candidate
names, turnout, malapportionment figures, and any claim that the official result has been
reproduced while the placeholder is in place.

## Product Principles

1. **Reproduction gates everything.** The engine must reproduce the official 2024
   allocation exactly before any interpretation is offered. When it cannot, the interface
   says so plainly rather than implying success. Never tune the engine to match the data.
2. **The counterfactual is mechanical, not predictive.** Changing a rule recomputes seats
   on fixed 2024 votes. It is arithmetic on a counterfactual rule, not a simulation of a
   counterfactual election, because voters and parties behave strategically. The interface
   states this whenever any control is off its 2024 default.
3. **No editorial verdict.** Report what the arithmetic produces. Never call a result
   unfair, never say a party was cheated, never recommend a threshold.
4. **Every number traces to a document.** Rules cite their pasal, figures cite their KPU
   source, and the citation is reachable in the interface rather than in a README.
5. **Party colours belong to the data, not the designer.** They are chosen against a
   measured contrast floor, and the interface itself carries no competing accent.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Lighthouse accessibility at or above 95; WCAG 2.1 A and AA verified with axe-core at
  380, 720 and 1400 px in both colour schemes.
- Every instrument has a keyboard-reachable table equivalent. The hemicycle is not the
  only way to get the numbers.
- Colour is never the only channel: party blocks carry labels, the changed-dapil marker is
  a shape rather than a tint.
- `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` collapses every interpolation to an immediate state
  write, and the cascade becomes steppable-only. Tested, not assumed.
- Focus is visible everywhere and never removed.
- Works on a phone; the hemicycle stays legible at 380 px.
